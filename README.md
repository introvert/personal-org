# Personal Org — Life & Portfolio Operating System

The personal counterpart to [`nightwatch-org`](https://github.com/kundi/nightwatch-org)
and [`formaviva-org`](https://github.com/introvert/formaviva-org).

Those repos answer _"what should the business do?"_ This one answers the
question that sits above both: **"what should the person running them do — with
their health, their attention, their two ventures, and their life?"**

It is a knowledge base, not a journal. Findings, decisions, frameworks, and a
plan — written so that decisions get made once and then executed, instead of
re-litigated every week.

## The thesis in one paragraph

Both business repos independently diagnose the same root cause — **breadth and
craft chosen over focus and distribution** ("a GTM failure, not a product
failure"; "effort pointed at the wrong problem"; "a big-company product surface
on a small-team budget"). That is not two coincidences; it is one decision-making
pattern, and it is currently expressed as one person carrying roughly four
companies' worth of operating surface with no health baseline and no confirmed
numbers. The fix is subtraction, in four moves: **adopt a value that limits scope,
get off the critical path at Nightwatch, cap Formaviva instead of pushing or
killing it, and treat health as infrastructure with an SLA.**

## Ground rules for this repo

- **Every claim about the businesses cites a source doc** in `nightwatch-org` or
  `formaviva-org`, so it can be argued with rather than absorbed.
- **Nothing here re-litigates a settled business decision.** Pricing (v4),
  positioning (Search Intelligence / Peec-as-tailwind), and Formaviva's one-web-stack
  call are closed. See [`05`](docs/05-decision-framework.md) §Standing decisions.
- **Health content is an operating protocol, not medical advice.** Anything
  involving symptoms or diagnosis belongs to a doctor.
- **This repo must not become a project.** It is ~12 short documents and a
  five-line weekly tracker. If it grows a tooling layer, it has become the very
  failure mode it was written to fix.

## Maintenance

estimate, a cap holds or breaks, a decision date arrives — update the relevant
doc and note the date. Cadence and drift signals are in
